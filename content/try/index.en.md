# Telegram (Main Channel)

Most of the day-to-day activity and support of the **Ubuntu Venezuela** community happens in our Telegram group.

- [Join the Telegram Group](https://t.me/ubuntuve)

In this group you will find:
- Community support for new and advanced users.
- News about Ubuntu releases.
- Coordination for local events and meetups.
- General free software conversations.

# IRC (Libera.chat)

If you prefer Ubuntu's traditional community chat, you can find us on the IRC network.

- **Server:** irc.libera.chat
- **Channel:** #ubuntu-ve
- **Ports:** 6667 (Standard), 6697 (TLS/SSL)

[Click here to join via web with Kiwi IRC](http://kiwiirc.com/client/irc.libera.chat/ubuntu-ve)

# Mailing Lists
You can also subscribe to our official mailing list for longer discussions or formal announcements.

- [Ubuntu Venezuela mailing list](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-ve)
