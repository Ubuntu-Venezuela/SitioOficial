# Get Involved in Translation
Ubuntu is an open source project and anyone can help improve the translation of the system interface and the applications included with it.
Your contribution helps many more users across Latin America and Spain use Ubuntu easily and accessibly in our language.

Rather than translating a lot at once, it is better to translate accurately and participate consistently, even with small contributions.

## How to start
You can participate by following these steps:

- [Create and log in to your Launchpad account](https://launchpad.net/+login)
- [Browse the package you would like to translate](https://translations.launchpad.net/ubuntu)
- Write the translation and submit a Suggestion
- Request or wait for the translation team to review your suggestions

# The Translation Team
You can join the official Spanish translation team once you meet the requirements set out in the team's rules to contribute more deeply.
