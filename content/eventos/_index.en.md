## Event organization

Ubuntu Venezuela organizes and takes part in encounters all over the country: free software installation festivals (FLISoL), workshops at educational institutions, virtual community meetings and anniversary celebrations.

If you would like to propose an activity, coordinate a workshop at your institution, or register for our next events, write to us at <junta@ubuntu-ve.org> or join our Telegram group.
