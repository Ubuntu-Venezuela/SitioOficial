## Local Community (LoCo Team)

1. **Participate actively**: introduce yourself in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/ubuntuve),
   the mailing list and the community forum.
2. **Contribute**: help with support, translations, events (FLISoL, install-fests)
   and projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/ubuntu-ve).
3. **Trust and consistency**: local membership is granted by the community to those who
   show sustained commitment to the team goals.

## Global Ubuntu Membership

Global membership is Canonical's official certification that you contribute to the
Ubuntu ecosystem. The process is described in the
[official Ubuntu wiki](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/Membership) and, for our community, in
[Ubuntu Venezuela Team](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/VenezuelaTeam).

### Already part of the team?

If you are an active community member, request your inclusion in the
[Members Directory](/miembros/ "Members Directory") to appear with your official profile,
badges and awards on this website.
