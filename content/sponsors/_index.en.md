## How to sponsor the community
You can sponsor the community by providing event venues, merchandise, refreshments, promotion, registration systems, or equipment for study groups and projects.

Recurring sponsors have their logo and detailed information featured on our official website and are presented at our in-person events, which helps give visibility to your institution or organization within the open source community in Venezuela.

If you would like to collaborate with us, write to our contact email or join our Telegram group.

- Email: junta@ubuntu-ve.org
